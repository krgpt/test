FAQ

Frequently asked questions

1. How is the AI training delivered?

Training is split into two tracks: beginner–intermediate and advanced. Venues may vary by weekday session, in meeting rooms for up to 10 people. Please bring your own laptop.

2. What outcomes can students expect after the training?

Beginner–Intermediate graduates

* Responsive websites: production landing pages and portfolio sites on a live URL

* Mobile apps: working apps integrated with databases and AI APIs

* 24/7 personal autonomous AI agent: a self-directed assistant that controls your PC and scrapes work data via messenger commands

Advanced graduates

* Enterprise AI Architecture Blueprint: an on-premises / commercial hybrid AI architecture tailored to your business

* AX ROI & Business Value Model: concrete ROI and TCO models that convince executives and boards

* AI Governance & Compliance Guide: company-specific AI security and governance guidelines covering global regulations (EU AI Act, etc.) and data-leak prevention

3. What does the enterprise AI and security consulting cover?

We mainly consult startups, SMBs, and mid-size companies on how to adopt AI systems for maximum impact. We also provide cybersecurity consulting that finds critical network vulnerabilities before they are exploited. Our team members operate at active white-hat hacker standard with outstanding RED-TEAM careers.

4. Do you also do solution development?

Development is not the largest part of our business, but consulting engagements sometimes require web or app development alongside. In those cases we deliver both together. We also take on development work when busy professionals have no time to build webs or apps themselves.
